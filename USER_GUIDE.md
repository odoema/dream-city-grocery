# Staff guide

## Signing in
Open the shop's web address, enter your e-mail and password. Forgot it? **Forgot password?** sends a reset link. On a phone, use the browser menu → *Add to Home screen* to install it like an app.

What you can see depends on your role (Owner, Manager, Accountant, Cashier).

## Sell
1. Search or **scan a barcode** (the scanner types the code and presses Enter — the item is added).
2. Tap products to add them. Weighed items ask for the quantity (e.g. 1.5 kg).
3. Change quantities with − / + or type them. Managers can change a price for this sale only.
4. Choose how the customer pays. **Credit** needs a customer and respects their credit limit.
5. For cash, type the amount given to see the change.
6. **Complete sale** → print the receipt if needed.

If the internet drops, the sale is **not** saved — the cart is kept; try again when back online. Pressing Complete twice never creates two sales.

Shortcuts on a computer: **F2** search, **F9** complete sale.

## Products
Add or edit products, prices and low-stock alerts. You cannot type a stock number: stock changes only when goods are sold, received, refunded or counted. For a brand-new product already on the shelf, open **History → Set opening stock** once.

## Customers
See who owes money. **Receive payment** when a customer pays back credit. Click a customer for their statement.

## Buy
- **Shopping list** — what to buy, based on what has been selling. Prepare the list, adjust quantities (give a reason), confirm, and optionally e-mail it to management.
- **Record a purchase** — after shopping: where you bought, what arrived, the cost, and whether it was paid now or owed. Stock goes up immediately.
- **Supplier bills** — what you still owe; record payments.
- **Orders** — optional formal purchase orders; receive them when goods arrive.

## Inventory (owner, manager, accountant)
- **Overview** — stock value at cost and selling price, plus what needs attention: out of stock, low, runs out soon, not selling, overstocked, priced below cost, missing price/cost. Tap a tile to list those products. **ABC**: A = the few products that make 80% of sales — never let them run out.
- **Adjust stock** — damaged, expired, lost, own use, found. Pick a reason, add products, enter how many. Losses go to "Stock loss" in Accounts. Large losses alert the owner.
- **History** — every stock movement (sales, purchases, refunds, counts, adjustments, returns) with who did it. Export to CSV.
- **Valuation** — stock value by category; export for the accountant.
- **Supplier returns** — send goods back; the supplier either credits what you owe or refunds you.
- **Labels** — print price and barcode labels (A4 sheet or a label printer). Products without a barcode get their SKU, which the till can scan.
- Owners get an alert when a product drops to its low-stock level or runs out.

## Count stock
1. Record any new deliveries first and pause selling.
2. **Start counting**, type each shelf count and press Enter (it saves immediately). Use 0 for none.
3. Review differences, then **Finish & post**. Stock and the books are corrected; the difference is shown as surplus or shortage.

## Reports
Now includes **Sales by item** and **by category** with profit and margin, exportable to CSV.
Sales, profit, payment methods, best sellers and staff totals for any period. Reprint receipts. Owners/managers can **refund** a whole sale (with a reason): stock returns and the accounts are reversed.

## Accounts (owner, manager, accountant)
Profit & loss, balance sheet and trial balance for any period, expenses, and the journal. **Record expense** for running costs (transport, rent, power, wages). Goods for resale are recorded under Buy, not as expenses.

## Staff & Settings (owner)
Change roles or switch off staff who leave. Settings holds the shop name, logo, currency, time zone, receipt text and printer width.

## Daily routine
- Morning: check **Home** for low stock; prepare the shopping list.
- After shopping: **Record a purchase**.
- Evening: compare **Cash taken** on Home with the cash drawer.
- Monthly: **Count stock**, review **Accounts**.
