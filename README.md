# Dream City Grocery

A mobile-friendly grocery Point of Sale and business management system for Dream City.

## Planned capabilities
- Point of sale and payments
- Product catalogue, SKU/barcodes and pricing
- Inventory and stock movements
- Suppliers and purchasing
- Customers and sales history
- Double-entry accounting
- Expenses and financial reporting
- Owner/manager/cashier roles
- Audit trail
- Dashboard and analytics
- PDF receipts and Google Drive archiving
- Email transaction alerts and receipts
- Mobile/PWA support

## Architecture
The application is designed around Supabase/PostgreSQL for transactional data and Google Apps Script for Google Workspace automation (Gmail, Drive and PDF receipts).

## Branding

## Currency

## Status
Starter/prototype. The next development milestone is connecting the UI to Supabase with authenticated, transactional sales and inventory workflows.
